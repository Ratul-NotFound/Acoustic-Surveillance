Slide 5

Research Gaps Identified

Gap 1: Limited Sound Classes
All systems classify only 2–5 classes. Real threats require comprehensive taxonomy.
Gap 2: No True Edge AI
High-accuracy models require GPU/server. Edge systems handle ≤3 classes.
Gap 3: No Solar Sustainability
Only 1 paper mentions solar; reports only 10 hrs battery life.
Gap 4: Single Communication
Systems use LoRa OR GSM, never both adaptively.
Gap 5: Analog Microphones
Prone to electrical noise; digital I2S is underutilized.
Gap 6: No PCEN
Per-Channel Energy Normalization (ideal for outdoor noise) never applied.

Model Selection — Why SE-DS-CNN?

ArchitectureParamsSize (INT8)InferenceAccuracySelected?
Standard 2D CNN~150K~150 KB~50 ms~82%❌ Too large
MobileNetV1~3.2M~3.2 MB~500 ms~92%❌ Far too large
SE-DS-CNN~28K27.4 KB9.5 ms88.21%✅ Best
1D CNN + LSTM~33K~33 KB~30 ms~89%❌ LSTM issue
Random ForestN/A~200 KB~5 ms~78%❌ Low accuracy

Depthwise-Separable Conv

8–9× fewer computations than standard convolution. Splits into depthwise (spatial) and pointwise (channel) operations.

Squeeze-and-Excitation

Channel attention mechanism — learns which frequency bands matter most per sound class. Adds only ~2 KB, improves accuracy 3–5%.

INT8 Quantization

4× compression (FP32→INT8). ESP32-S3 has hardware-accelerated INT8 vector instructions. Accuracy loss < 1%.
